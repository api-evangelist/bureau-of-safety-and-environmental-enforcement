MMA Monitors Gulf of America Oil and Gas Activities in Response to Hurricane Isaias NEW ORLEANS - The Marine Minerals Administration is monitoring offshore oil and gas operators in the Gulf of America as they evacuate platforms and rigs in response to the storm. The Marine Minerals Administration team will work with offshore operators and other state and federal agencies until operations return to normal, and the storm is no longer a threat to Gulf of America oil and gas activities. Based on data from offshore operator reports submitted as of 11:00 a.m.
